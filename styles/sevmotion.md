# SevMotion (@SevMotion): Measured Style Guide

Measured from viral Short `aMLDjErWPZY` ("What If the Moon Disappeared?") via `scripts/measure.py` (numbers.json) + 30 fps contact sheet analysis.

---

## 1. Format & Overview
| Rule | Target (Measured) |
|---|---|
| Frame | 9:16 vertical (1080×1920), 30 fps |
| Length | 54.8 seconds |
| Theme | "What If" hypothetical sci-fi & cosmic curiosity |

---

## 2. Pacing & Camera Mechanics
| Metric | Target (Measured) |
|---|---|
| **Cuts per minute** | **30.7 cuts/min** (avg shot length **1.89s**) |
| **Hero Camera Move** | **Extreme Macro Punch-In**: Cut starts on a wide cosmic balcony (moon in sky), rushes into a 3/4 medium angle, then cuts into an extreme macro close-up of the character's eye (showing the celestial reflection inside the pupil) within 1.0s. |
| **Camera motion index** | **0.049** |
| **Holds & Blinks** | Macro eye holds for 6–10 frames, character blinks, then cuts to the world-scale impact. |

---

## 3. Light & Color Palette
| Metric | Target (Measured) |
|---|---|
| **Shadow Hue & RGB** | Mean Hue **237°** (midnight space blue: `RGB(6, 7, 37)`) |
| **Highlight Hue & RGB** | Mean Hue **43°** (celestial starlight gold / warm glow: `RGB(249, 231, 187)`) |
| **Saturation** | **0.60** (vibrant, cosmic color palette) |
| **Pure black (`pure_black_pct`)** | **6.17%** (deep void of space and nighttime skies) |

---

## 4. Character Anatomy & Expressions
| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Head & Eyes** | Smooth peach/skin tone head (`#F8C8A0`) with **giant expressive black oval anime eyes** containing high-contrast white specular reflection dots (or environmental reflections like the moon). |
| **Costume** | Signature vibrant wardrobe (emerald green knitted beanie with white puff ball, lavender/purple sweater). |
| **Expressions** | Raised curved black eyebrows, blinks with smooth eyelid arcs, subtle mouth curvature. |

---

## 5. Captions & Typography
| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Style** | ALL-CAPS bold rounded sans-serif font (`WHAT`, `IF THE`, `MOON`, `DISAPPEARED?`) |
| **Border & Contrast** | Thick black exterior stroke (3–4px) ensuring legibility over bright planetary visuals or dark space backdrops. |
| **Position** | Placed at **~60%–65% frame height**, 1–2 words per beat. |
