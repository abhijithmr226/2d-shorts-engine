# Kyū-Shirataki Station: The Viral Myth vs The Truth

## The Story Formula
- **Hook (0:00 - 0:08):** A surprising paradox that grabs immediate attention — "People all over the world cried over this train station. And the story they cried about wasn't true."
- **The Setup & The Viral Tale (0:08 - 0:52):** A lone wooden station in Hokkaido, Japan. A high school girl, Kana Harada, using it every morning. The viral internet claim that JR Hokkaido kept an unprofitable station open just for her until graduation day.
- **The Pivot (0:52 - 1:07):** "You see, the railway never kept that station open for her."
- **The Payoff / Reality (1:07 - 2:00):** 
  - Closure was scheduled in July 2015, months before the viral post, as part of 4 rural station decommissionings.
  - Japan's timetable revisions always happen every March; schools also end in March. The correlation was purely coincidental.
  - The train was far from empty — dozens of fellow high school students were already aboard. Kana herself stated the station simply let her sleep a bit longer in the morning.
- **The Melancholic Aftermath & Twist (2:00 - 2:14):** Demolished in October. The ending loop question: "If it was never true, why did so many of us believe it so fast?"
