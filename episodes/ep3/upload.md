### Description
Picture the Monopoly Man. Top hat, mustache... monocle? If you said yes, you're not alone. But he has never worn one in 90 years. 

From Nelson Mandela to Pikachu's tail and the Fruit of the Loom cornucopia, here is the real neuroscience behind the world's most famous false memories.
